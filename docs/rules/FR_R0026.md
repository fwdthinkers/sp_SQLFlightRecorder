## What it detects

Always-emitted coverage and capability summary: snapshot count, skipped collectors, suppressed rules, capability flags. Also carries graded gap findings (D-066).

## How it is computed

Repository-only (D-014/D-081). Aggregates the window's snapshot count, skipped/partial collector steps, suppressed rules, and capability posture. Cannot be disabled (D-098).
